music

since about 2015 i’ve been making a variety of music under a number of different names and aliases, each with their own styles and intentions, most of which can be found and downloaded on my Bandcamp page .

below is a more comprehensive list of all of my projects and releases:

rabbit and company (tentative name)

personal thoughts and surreal stories expressed as a noisy, lo-fi, emo love letter to bands and genres i am deeply indebted to. not a lot released under this one yet, but currently it’s my main musical project and i’m excited to eventually put those songs out.

  • album (WIP)
  • EP (WIP)
  • idk what im doing!!! (2021)

DREAM DISPHORIA

an unabashedly frenzied and edgy project that revels in its own absurdity. a partly tongue-in-cheek, partly sincere exploration of negative emotions and dysphoria. breakcore-adjacent, mathcore-adjacent, harsh noise-adjacent. home to Lorna, the girl with a hole in her head.

  • EP (WIP)
  • intrusyve thots (2023)
  • complete dyssociation; we never needed this body anyways (2022)

all the tree tops

initially an experiment in combining the structures and rhythms of math rock and midwest emo with the sound-palette of EDM and electronica.

  • b-sides circa 2021 (2022)
  • the runaway! ep (2019)
  • morning song (2020)
  • time spent / thinking of you (2020)
  • it’s always better (with you around) (2019)
  • snowflake (2019)

crystal clouds

once my main musical outlet, crystal clouds was a dreamy, lo-fi, bedroom pop-ish project, swimming in cute synths, acoustic guitars and ambient textures. i made a lot of this music in my early and mid teens.

  • drifting off (2022)
  • overly sentimental (a collection of recordings from 2017–2021) (2021)
  • april 12, 2021 (2021)
  • sleepless (thanks for keeping me up at night) (2018)
  • things to remember (2017)
  • i like you // i hate myself (2017)
  • one giant love note ft. frost (2017)
  • summer’s been nice (2017)

we should talk again

various beats, instrumentals, and doodles mainly from 2019 to 2021 during a big creative rut of mine where i took a break from any big personal projects. acts as a kind of archive or time capsule. i’ve been thinking of compiling a second volume for this one some time.

  • goodie bag mixtape (2021)

LANPARTY
