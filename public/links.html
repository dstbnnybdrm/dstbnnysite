linking to the work of others is a way of celebrating their achievements and, by doing so, gaining dignity for yourself and your work. you deserve to be part of the web, not its final destination.

had enough of this place? here’s an assortment of links to other sites you should consider visiting! (note that, just like this site, i can’t and won’t guarantee that any of these sites will be child-friendly!)
