about

profile

hi! you can call me JP, i’m a creative rabbit!

i’m mainly a multi-genre musician, producer, and programmer, but i also occasionally dip into writing, video editing, visual art, and of course, webmastery. i also have ADHD and amniotic band syndrome (ABS).

i love a lot of things; i’m not really concerned about having any specific style or fitting in any kind of box or anything, i just make what i like, whatever feels right, or honest, or just fun! i love learning and approach most things with a pretty DIY mindset. i don’t really mind if things come out less than perfect as a result, it’s more human (rabbit?) and personal that way.

i own a few different kinds of instruments but i’d mostly call myself a guitarist just faking it on everything else lol. guitar is where i feel right at home, musically, other than a DAW. i play lefty despite being right-handed because of my ABS—it changes how i have to approach many things actually, but i make do!
