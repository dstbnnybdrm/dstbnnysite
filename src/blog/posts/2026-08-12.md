last one so maybe i just make it a hard rule for myself to stop doing that

periods at a time when needed. i can get up from the computer whenever and this
place will still be here as long as i want it to.

i don't really care for formalities at the moment, i kind of want to just write
words down so the words exist, so i'll just blabber on briefly about life
lately, maybe scatter in some demos wherever? (volume warning though, browsers

at the time of posting the site has recently gone through another rework! i
actually pretty much reworked everything from the ground up, which is why it
took some time (along with all the other stuff i’ve been doing). i wanted to
make sure everything still worked and was functional and readable when there
aren’t any styles or scripts, so for the first couple weeks i was just staring
at that classic default HTML look, which, honestly? kinda grew on me lol. idk,
there’s a charm to it, maybe i’ll make a theme where other than the layout it’s
all just browser defaults.

this time around i finally bit the bullet and learned how to use a static site
generator, specifically [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/)! i typically like
doing things as simply as possible and even though this site is far from complex
it was already getting to the point where it was becoming tiring for me to work
on. i was doing things in pretty inefficient ways, lots of manual copying and
pasting from file to file to file, which isn’t very fun and creative, just
annoying lol. thankfully though, now that i let Eleventy handle all that stuff,
i’ve been having a lot more fun with the site! right now it’s still a little
barebones when compared to before the rework, but hopefully that’ll change as
time goes on!

as far as music goes, it’s been a lot more collaborative for me lately! jamming
with [friends](/links.html#friends) in real life is a really special experience.
super fun and i love the magical moments that just can happen by accident. i’ve
also taken up producing and/or mixing a few projects for some people _other
than_ my friend and frequent collaborator Dan (i.e. Mahal Slick, mwah!); it’s
always an honour when someone else trusts you enough to put your fingerprints on
their work.

in my time away from the site i practised my mixing a bunch, actually. i never
really _intentionally_ practised mixing before, the practice came from just
working on the current song i had to work on. but in my free time away from
classes i wouldn’t have the creative or emotional energy to, like, start a new
song or anything, but i guess i had enough to mix? some sites have free
multi-tracks they put up just for others to practice with, which is super cool
and generous, so i used a few of those to practice with. i’ll probably link
those in the resources page when i finally get to it!

and of course, i’ve still been doing [original music](/projects/music/). i’ve
been taking a teeny break from the album i had in the works but i’ve got a
little EP of songs that didn’t fit on it to come out before it. these are both
for my current main project which will be a kind of emo noise pop thing! i’m
embarrassingly still undecided on the name for the project, but i’ve been
tentatively calling it _rabbit and company_ here on the site. obviously all my
music is representative of me in some way, but this project in particular is
_very me_, if that makes any sense lol. i’m excited to share it eventually!

i’ve also been getting into making harsh noise music lol and i have a few
experiments that might make it onto a little
[DREAM DISPHORIA](/projects/music/#dream-disphoria) release sometime :3c? for me
harsh noise is akin to, like, ambient or drone music, and for as _abrasive_ as
it can be it’s also a weirdly relaxing experience—the process of making noise
and just exploring the sounds can be straight up meditative. i guess that isn’t
too surprising though, i’ve always had a propensity for lo-fi and distortion and
loud, noisy shit.
