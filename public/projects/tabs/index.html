tabs

sometimes i write out guitar tabs for songs that i love but don’t have many/any. it’s been great practice for my ears and also weirdly fun and almost meditative? i figure them out by ear, close investigative examinations of live videos (if i can find any), or sometimes dissecting them within a DAW by using EQ and isolating channels, et cetera.

i’ve been posting them on Ultimate Guitar Tabs but i figured i might as well put them here too! you can click on a link to view it or right-click and “save link as…” to download the text file on your own device!

and yes, i do like Weatherday a lot, lmao.
